Placeholder. This is the first assessment: pick a published FID figure, rebuild
enough of the instrument to compute it yourself, and report what you get.

The mark is not in matching the number. It is in the gap. Almost nobody closes
it completely, and the interesting work is naming precisely which choices your
number and theirs did not share — sample size, reference set, resize filter,
which activation layer, which weights.

Week 5 explains why the gap is not noise, and why the standard deviations you
compute will not warn you about it.
