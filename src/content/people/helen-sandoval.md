Helen Sandoval convenes SLOP8412 and holds a continuing appointment in the
School of Continuous Improvement. Her work concerns the estimation of
distances between distributions from finite samples, and the reporting habits
that grow up around any number once it appears in a table.

She has not reported a score to a second decimal place since 2019, and
considers the first one negotiable.
