Replacing the number does not remove the obligation to defend it. The final
report must apply the same scrutiny to its own proposal that the course
applied to FID.

## Bring the evidence, including the gaps

Bring the measurement log, a candidate reporting table and your strongest
reason for retaining FID. Separate measurements you have made from studies
you would still need to run. Week 9's human-evaluation protocol belongs in
that distinction too.

## The planned lecture

Audit a proposed reporting table against the earlier weeks. Check its
sampling conditions and its representation choices. For each interpretation,
ask what observation supports it and what counterexample would defeat it.
End by defending the condition under which keeping FID remains useful.

Use the [week 11 session](/sessions/11-why-it-persists/) to prepare the case
for comparability, then test your proposal against the
[final report requirements](/assessments/final-report/).

## Carry it into the defence

Leave with an annotated reporting table and a named limitation, not a claim
that the replacement has solved evaluation. The
[week 12 presentations](/sessions/12-what-you-would-report-instead/) remain
the place to defend the proposal and score the bench one last time.
