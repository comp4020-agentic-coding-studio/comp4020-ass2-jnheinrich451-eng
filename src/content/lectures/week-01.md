The session tells you to score A and B against R and record what you get.
This page is the same exercise with the waiting removed, on a bench small
enough that your browser is the laptop.

Set the candidate, press score, and read two numbers: the true distance from
the closed form, and the estimate computed from the samples you just drew.
Press draw again and the estimate moves while the truth does not. Raise d
toward 64 and the two stop being on speaking terms.

Everything this page computes runs in your browser through the same
arithmetic the course pins in `spec/`, and nothing you press leaves the page.
The reason the gap behaves the way it does is week 5's whole subject; this
page only lets you meet it early.
