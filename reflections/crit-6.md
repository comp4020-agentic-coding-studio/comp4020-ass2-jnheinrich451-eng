# Crit 6 — the Assignment 2 retro

The rules say assignment repos carry no reflection, and the retro presents
from `PROCESS.md`. I know that; this entry is voluntary. I answered the two
standing prompts anyway because I answered them every other week, and the
habit is the point.

**What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?**

Honestly, nothing I would call a breakthrough. What moved the work was a loop,
run many times: plan, restrict, implement, check, report, then modify or
proceed. The recursive part was tracking, every round, what was finished and
what was still planned. The checking part took the most time, and it deserved
to. What made the loop work at all was stating the intention and the
requirement clearly before anything got built; when a round went wrong, the
cause was usually an instruction of mine that could be read two ways.

**What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?**

It convinced me that this loop is the average case of software engineering,
not something special to agents. Know the requirement clearly, implement it,
verify by testing, then decide whether it is okay or needs another round. The
deciding is the part I underrated before: some issues we fixed, some we
tolerated on purpose at that stage, and knowing which is which turned out to
be the actual job. I want to be the developer who runs this loop deliberately,
not the one who waits for a trick.
